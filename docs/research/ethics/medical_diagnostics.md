# Medical Astrology and Diagnostic Taxonomies

## Objective

To bridge the gap between traditional Medical Jyotish and modern clinical diagnostics through structured mapping to the International Classification of Diseases (ICD-10) codes.

## Biological Humor (Tridosha) Mapping

Quantifying the biological constitution (Prakriti) and imbalance (Vikriti) via planetary weighting.

| Humor             | Primary Planets               | Sign Correspondences               |
| :---------------- | :---------------------------- | :--------------------------------- |
| **Vata (Air)**    | Saturn, Rahu, Mercury (mixed) | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius, Capricorn |
| **Pitta (Fire)**  | Sun, Mars, Ketu               | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius, Scorpio   |
| **Kapha (Water)** | Moon, Venus, Jupiter          | Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Pisces      |

## Diagnostic Mapping (ICD-10)

High-precision diagnostics require a relational database linking planetary afflictions $(planet, sign, house, aspect)$ to specific pathological outcomes.

### Case Study: Cardiovascular Pathology

- **Primary Indicators:** Sun (Vitality), 4th House (Chest), Leo (Heart).
- **Affliction Logic:**
  - `IF (Sun in 6th/8th) AND (Mars/Rahu aspects 4th House) AND (Leo lord is debilitated)`
  - `THEN Flag Potential Risk: ICD-10 I20-I25 (Ischemic Heart Diseases)`

## Epidemiological Validation

Astro-Fusion establishes a machine learning model that cross-references anonymized user pathological histories with natal chart data to identify statistically significant correlations (p-value < 0.05). This serves as the foundation for the project's empirical claims.
