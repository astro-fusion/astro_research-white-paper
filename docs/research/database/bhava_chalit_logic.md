# Bhava Chalit and Spatial Discrepancies

## The Dissonance Problem

In high-latitude births, the Equal House or Whole Sign systems often obscure the physical reality of celestial space. A planet may appear in the 5th Sign (Rasi) but mathematically occupy the 4th House $(Bhava)$ based on its degree of declination and the rotation of the Earth.

## Algorithmic Interpretation Logic

When a planet shifts between its Rasi placement and its Bhava placement, the interpretive engine applies the following weighting ratio:

1.  **Qualitative Results (Personality/Mood):** Weighted 30% toward the Rasi placement.
2.  **Quantitative Results (Physical Manifestation/Events):** Weighted 70% toward the Bhava Chalit placement.

## Calculation Methods

The platform allows toggling between major house systems to analyze variance:

- **Placidus:** Time-based spatial division.
- **Sri Pati:** Midpoint-based division common in Vedic traditions.
- **Whole Sign:** Symbolic alignment (Sign = House).

## Spatial Mapping Matrix

The engine generates a 12-node matrix where each node represents a house with variable degree-lengths. This matrix is then superimposed onto the fixed 30° zodiacal backdrop to trigger "House Migration" flags in the interpretation engine.
