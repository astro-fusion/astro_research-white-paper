## 📊 Key Research Findings

### Research Question
**"Is there a correlation between Vedic Astrology planetary strength and Vedic Numerology values?"**

### Answer
**NO - The systems show negligible correlation (near 0.0)**

### Why This Matters

1. **Temporal Mismatch:**
   - Astrology: 8,760 changes per year
   - Numerology: ~73 changes per year
   - Ratio: 120:1 frequency difference

2. **Different Calculation Methods:**
   - Astrology: Based on astronomical positions (continuous)
   - Numerology: Based on date arithmetic (discrete)

3. **Independent Systems:**
   - Low correlation coefficients validate this
   - Traditional approach of using both separately is justified
   - Each system provides unique, non-redundant insights

### Practical Implication
**Use both systems together** - they complement each other because they measure different aspects of cosmic influence.
