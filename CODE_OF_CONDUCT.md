# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the Vedic Astrology Research Platform pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and research approaches
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism of research methodology or code
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the integrity of research
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Acknowledging the difference between personal beliefs and empirical conclusions

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal/political attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive comments, especially in research discussions
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Using this research platform to promote unsupported predictions, scams, or pseudoscience without empirical grounding
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## 🔬 Research Integrity Standards

This project is an empirical research platform. We hold ourselves to additional standards:

- **Intellectual Honesty**: Report findings faithfully, including null results and failures to reproduce
- **Reproducibility**: All research claims must be linked to reproducible code and data
- **Open Falsification**: Welcome challenges to hypotheses — disproving a claim is as valuable as proving one
- **Source Transparency**: Cite data sources, algorithms, and prior work accurately

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and any other official forums — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at **bishal.ghimire@gmail.com**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Violation Level       | Community Impact                                           | Consequence                                              |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 - Correction**    | Inappropriate language or minor unprofessional behavior    | Private written warning; public apology may be requested |
| **2 - Warning**       | Single serious incident or pattern of violations           | Temporary ban from interaction (duration specified)      |
| **3 - Temporary Ban** | Serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior      | Temporary ban from all community interaction             |
| **4 - Permanent Ban** | Pattern of violations, harassment, or threatening behavior | Permanent ban                                            |
