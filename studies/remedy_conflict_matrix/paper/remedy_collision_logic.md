# Remedy Collision and Conflict Resolution

## The Remediation Paradox

Strengthening a planet through a gemstone (e.g., Yellow Sapphire for Jupiter) increases its functional energy. If that planet rules a malefic house (e.g., the 6th or 8th) for the user's ascendant, the remedy may trigger unintended negative consequences (e.g., health issues or accidents).

## Collision Detection Algorithm

The system runs a **Impact Simulation** before authorizing a remedy:

1.  **Primary Target:** Identify the planet to be strengthened ($P_{target}$).
2.  **House Lordship Audit:** Determine the houses ruled by $P_{target}$ in D-1 and D-9.
3.  **Side-Effect Calculation:**
    - If $P_{target}$ rules $\{6, 8, 12\}$, calculate the **Malefic Coefficient** ($C_m$).
    - If $C_m > \text{Threshold}$, flag a "High Risk" collision.
4.  **Neutralization Check:** Scan for conflicting aspects. If strengthening $P_{target}$ also aspects a sensitive point (Marma) or an afflicted natal planet, the risk score is further weighted.

## Conflict Resolution Protocols

- **Substitution:** If a gemstone is too risky, suggest a **Mantra** or **Dana** (donation), which modulates energy without structural amplification.
- **Color Therapy Balance:** Use specific color frequencies to neutralize the side-effects of a primary remedy.
- **Timing (Muhurat):** Authorize the remedy only when the dasha/transit environment is supportive.
