# Side-Effect Analysis and Predictive Impact

## Simulation of Strengthening Remedies

When a user implements a remedy (e.g., wearing a gemstone), the system models the shift in the chart's equilibrium.

### Algorithmic Impact Projection

1.  **Node Amplification:** Increase the weight of the target planet ($P_{target}$) in the Yoga detection graph.
2.  **Edge Recalculation:** Re-run aspectual and lordship logic with the amplified $P_{target}$.
3.  **House Result Re-Scoring:**
    - For all 12 houses, calculate the new **House Potency** ($H_i'$).
    - $H_i' = H_i + \Delta I(P_{target})$, where $\Delta I$ is the influence delta.

## Predictive Risk Matrix

The system outputs a risk-benefit heat map:

- **Green (Benefit):** Significant improvement in house matters without negative side-effects.
- **Yellow (Caution):** Improvement in primary house, but minor stress in secondary houses (e.g., increased wealth but minor health issues).
- **Red (Collision):** Significant risk of collateral damage; remedy implementation is discouraged.
