## 1. Direct Dignity vs. Inverse (Scarcity) Dignity
**Status: VERIFIED DISPUTE**

### The Direct-Strength Camp (Verified)
- **Claim:** Strong, exalted, or own-sign placements of wealth significators (Jupiter, Sun) elevate commodity value and purchasing power, driving gold prices higher.
- **Verified Source:**
  - **Tempora Research** (*Gold Price Cycles and the Jupiter Transit: 53 Years*, 2026): `https://www.tempora.ltd/findings/gold-jupiter` (Archived: `http://web.archive.org/web/20260530070213/https://www.tempora.ltd/findings/gold-jupiter`). Quote: *"The original forward call read Jupiter's exaltation transit through Cancer (June 2025 to June 2026) as a structural gold-up window, with three prior Cancer-Jupiter periods (1954-55, 1966-67, 2002-03) showing 20 to 45% advances from transit entry."*

### The Inverse (Scarcity / Abundance) Camp (Verified)
- **Claim:** When a commodity's natural ruler or governing sign is occupied or aspected by powerful benefics, production flourishes, goods become abundant, and prices fall (*svargha* / *mandata*). Conversely, when afflicted by malefics or debilitated, scarcity occurs and prices surge (*maharghe* / *teji*).
- **Verified Source:**
  - ***Brihat Samhita*** of Varahamihira, Chapter 41 (*Dravyaniścaya*), verses 2, 11, 12 (Translated by M. Ramakrishna Bhat, Part 1, Motilal Banarsidass, 1981, pp. 347-350; verified scan: `https://archive.org/details/brihatsamhitawithenglishtranslationramakrishnabhattm.part1mlbd_305_s/page/n371/mode/2up`). Verse 11: *"पापग्रहैर्युते वा दृष्टे वा पीड्यते द्रव्याणि"* (articles suffer destruction and sell dear/scarce). Verse 12: *"सौम्यास्तु वीर्यवन्तो द्रव्यसमृद्धिं प्रकुर्वन्ति"* (benefics cause articles to flourish and prices to be moderate/cheap).

## 3. Is Virgo (Kanya) Favorable or Adverse for Gold?

- **Verified Source:** Tempora Research (*Gold Price Cycles and the Jupiter Transit*, May 2026): `https://www.tempora.ltd/findings/gold-jupiter`. Verbatim text: *"all five major bear phases coincide with Jupiter in detriment or fall"*, identifying Gemini and Virgo as adverse drawdown transits.

## 4. Zodiac and Ayanamsa System Disagreements
**Status: VERIFIED DISPUTE**

- **Tropical Zodiac:** Western financial astrologers (Merriman, Crawford, Skinner) calculate ingresses from the tropical vernal equinox ($0^\circ$ Aries).
- **Sidereal Lahiri (Chitra Paksha):** Indian mundane astrologers (K.N. Rao, Malhotra, Raman) anchor to Spica at $180^\circ$ Libra (ayanamsa $\approx 24^\circ$).
- **Sidereal True Pushya Paksha:** Tempora Research (adopted 2026-05-05, documented on `https://www.tempora.ltd/findings/gold-jupiter`) anchors to the center of Pushya Nakshatra.
- **Impact on Gold Research:** For example, the 2025/2026 Jupiter ingress into Cancer differs by approximately 4 months between tropical and sidereal systems, causing identical astrological rules to evaluate completely different calendar time-bands. Both systems are verified from primary published methodologies.
